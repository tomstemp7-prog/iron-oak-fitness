The Iron & Oak Difference

Everything You Need to Thrive

We've built more than a gym. Iron & Oak is a space designed around you — your performance, your recovery, your community.

World-Class Facilities

Over 400 pieces of premium equipment, a 25-metre pool, spa suites, and six studio spaces — all under one roof.

Expert Personal Training

Our certified coaches bring expertise in strength, sport, rehabilitation and nutrition — crafting programmes that deliver real results.

Spa & Recovery

Restore and recharge in our Finnish sauna, steam room, ice bath, and tranquil relaxation suite — recovery is part of every membership.

Community & Family

A warm, inclusive environment where beginners and athletes alike feel at home. Family memberships, kids' classes, and social events throughout the year.
