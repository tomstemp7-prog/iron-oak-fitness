How it started

BUILT BY TRAINERS, FOR EVERYONE

Iron & Oak was founded in 2019 by Marcus Webb and Jade Osei — two personal trainers tired of corporate gym chains that prioritised profit over people. They wanted a space where every member felt seen, where equipment was maintained, and where coaching actually mattered.

Starting from a 800sqm unit on 12 Caversham Road, Reading, they built a gym from the ground up — sourcing quality kit, training a passionate team, and building relationships with every member who walked through the door.

Five years later, Iron & Oak has grown to over 1,200 members, six expert coaches, and a reputation as Reading’s best independent gym. The mission hasn’t changed: help real people build their best selves, without the BS.
