Strength & Conditioning

Gym Floor

Over 400 pieces of industry-leading equipment spread across two levels. Free weights up to 60kg, multiple squat racks, a full cable and machine zone, and a dedicated functional training rig — all sourced from Life Fitness, Technogym, and Eleiko.

The cardio deck spans 80 treadmills, bikes, rowers, and ski ergs, each with personal entertainment screens.

  • ✓ 400+ pieces of equipment
  • ✓ Free weights up to 60kg
  • ✓ Life Fitness & Technogym cardio
  • ✓ Dedicated functional rig
  • ✓ Expert floor staff always on hand

Aquatics

Swimming Pool

Our 25-metre, six-lane competition-spec pool is maintained at 29°C with lane ropes in place at all times. Adjacent is our 12-person hydrotherapy pool heated to 36°C with twelve water jet stations.

  • ✓ 25-metre, 6-lane competition pool
  • ✓ Maintained at 29°C
  • ✓ 12-person hydrotherapy pool at 36°C
  • ✓ Lane swimming all day
  • ✓ Poolside loungers (Premium)

Recovery & Wellbeing

Spa & Sauna

Built around the Nordic concept of contrast therapy. Our 90°C Finnish sauna seats twelve, the steam room operates at 45°C with eucalyptus infusion, and the ice bath is held at 10°C. A private relaxation room with heated loungers completes the suite.

  • ✓ 90°C Finnish sauna (12 seats)
  • ✓ Eucalyptus steam room at 45°C
  • ✓ Ice bath plunge pool at 10°C
  • ✓ Heated relaxation lounge
  • ✓ Robes & towels provided (Club & Premium)

Group Fitness

Group Studios

Three dedicated studios host 40+ classes per week. Studio One is 280 sq metres with a sprung hardwood floor and professional sound system. Studio Two is our 30-bike cycling studio with immersive projection. Studio Three is our yoga and pilates space.

  • ✓ 3 dedicated studios
  • ✓ 40+ classes per week
  • ✓ Sprung hardwood floors in Studio 1
  • ✓ 30-bike cycling studio
  • ✓ Book via app up to 7 days ahead

Racket Sports

Tennis & Padel Courts

Four floodlit all-weather courts — two full-size tennis courts and two padel courts. LTA-accredited coaches offer individual lessons, group coaching, and internal leagues across all ability levels.

  • ✓ 2 full-size tennis courts
  • ✓ 2 padel courts
  • ✓ Floodlit for evening play
  • ✓ LTA-accredited coaching
  • ✓ Racket hire at no charge
