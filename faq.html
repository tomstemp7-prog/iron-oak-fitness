Memberships & Contracts

No joining fee, ever. Your first payment is simply your first month’s or year’s membership — nothing more.

Facilities

Yes — we have 30 free on-site parking spaces available 24/7. No permit required.
Yes. Day-use lockers are available at no cost — just bring your own padlock. If you’d like a permanent locker, these can be rented from £5/month. Speak to reception to arrange this.
Towels are not included in membership but you can hire one for £1 per session, or purchase a branded Iron & Oak towel from our reception desk.

Classes

Booking is strongly recommended via the member app as classes fill up quickly. Plus members can book 7 days ahead; Premium members enjoy 10-day advance booking priority.
Please cancel at least 2 hours before the class start time via the app so your spot can be released to others. Repeated no-shows (3 or more in a month) may result in a temporary booking restriction.
No. Basic members can attend any class as a drop-in for £5 per session. Classes are unlimited and fully included in Plus and Premium memberships.

Absolutely. All of our classes are coached and can be scaled to your fitness level. Just let the instructor know before the session that you’re new and they’ll look after you.

Getting Started

We offer a free 30-minute gym induction for all new members — highly recommended but not mandatory. It covers safe use of equipment, our facilities, and how to get the most out of your membership.
Trainers, comfortable workout clothes, a water bottle, and a padlock for your locker. Everything else you need — kit, supplements, and smoothies — is available on-site.
Plus members get 2 guest passes per month; Premium members get 4. Basic members can bring guests at £10 per visit. All guests must be accompanied by their member host at all times.
Yes! Try the gym completely free for 3 days — no card required, no obligation. Just pop in to reception with some ID and we’ll get you sorted.
The gym is open 24/7 for all members with keycard access. Staffed hours are Mon–Fri 06:00–21:00, and Sat–Sun 07:00–18:00.

Payment

Monthly memberships are billed on the same date each month as your join date. Annual memberships are billed in full upfront.
Yes — annual membership saves you the equivalent of 2 months’ fees. You can pay upfront by card or bank transfer. See our Memberships page for annual pricing.
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and direct debit for memberships. Cash is accepted for in-gym purchases (smoothies, merchandise, towel hire) but not for memberships.
Monthly fees are non-refundable once charged. If you’re unable to use the gym, we’d recommend freezing your membership instead. Speak to reception for help.
Yes — we offer 15% off any membership plan with a valid student ID or NHS badge. Just show your ID at reception when signing up. The discount applies to all plan tiers.
