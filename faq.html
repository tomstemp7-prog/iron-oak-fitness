Frequently Asked Questions

Membership & Contracts

Yes. Monthly memberships can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice via the member app, by email to hello@ironoak.co.uk, or in person at reception. Your membership remains fully accessible during the notice period. No cancellation fees on monthly plans.

No joining fee on any membership tier. Monthly plans are contract-free with no minimum term beyond the first month. Annual plans are voluntary and offer the equivalent of two months free. Our philosophy: people stay because they love the club, not because they feel trapped.

Yes — all members can freeze for up to three consecutive months per calendar year for medical reasons, extended travel, or personal circumstances. Billing pauses and your end date extends accordingly. Request via the app or email hello@ironoak.co.uk with at least seven days' notice before your next billing date.

Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. All changes via the app, by phone on 0118 496 2200, or in person. No fees for changing plans.

Yes. Corporate memberships for businesses of 3+ employees — contact corporate@ironoak.co.uk for a tailored proposal. Family memberships include joint adult plans and junior add-ons (under 16) with access to supervised sessions and family swim times.

Facilities

Monday to Friday: 06:00 – 22:00. Saturday and Sunday: 07:00 – 20:00. Bank Holidays: 08:00 – 18:00. Last entry to the spa is 30 minutes before closing time.

Yes — 80 dedicated spaces, all free for members during visits. Accessible 24 hours via your member keycard. Secure cycle storage, a bike repair station, and 6 EV charging points also available.

Club and Premium members receive complimentary towel service. Essential members can hire towels for £1 or bring their own. Day-use lockers available throughout changing rooms on a first-come basis, requiring a £1 coin deposit (returned on exit). Changing rooms include hairdryers and complimentary toiletries.

Spa access (sauna, steam room, ice bath, relaxation lounge) is included with Club and Premium memberships. Essential members can access as a day pass add-on for £12 per visit. During peak times a timed booking system operates, bookable 24 hours ahead via the app.

Yes — the Iron & Oak Member Café serves hot and cold drinks, protein shakes, smoothies, and light food. Menu designed with our nutrition team to support training goals. Members may also bring their own food. Premium members receive complimentary coffee in the Business Lounge.

Classes

Unlimited group fitness classes are included with Club and Premium memberships. Essential members pay £8 per class. Club members can book 7 days ahead; Premium members get a 10-day priority booking window.

Primarily through the Iron & Oak member app (iOS and Android) — browse the timetable, filter by class or instructor, and book in seconds. Also bookable at reception. Popular sessions fill 48 hours ahead. A 2-hour cancellation policy applies; three late cancellations in 30 days results in a 7-day booking suspension.

Yes — almost all classes are designed for a range of abilities. Instructors offer modifications and progressions within the same session. Arrive five minutes early if it's your first time so the instructor can set you up correctly. Our Foundations classes are specifically designed for newcomers.

Please cancel at least two hours before the session so waitlisted members can take your place. Three late cancellations or no-shows in a 30-day period results in a seven-day booking suspension, after which privileges are automatically restored.

Yes. Eight qualified PTs on site, ranging from strength specialists to sports rehabilitation coaches. Essential members pay standard rate; Club members get 10% off; Premium members include one monthly PT session and receive 20% off additional sessions.

Getting Started

Absolutely — complimentary tours run seven days a week during staffed hours, covering all facilities. Around 45 minutes, no obligation to join. Book via the Contact page, call 0118 496 2200, or simply walk in. We also offer a free guest day pass.

Not compulsory but strongly recommended, especially for those newer to training. The free 45-minute induction covers equipment safety, the app, facility tour, and a brief coach consultation about your goals. Bookable when you join or at any time.

Training clothing and clean indoor-soled trainers (outdoor footwear not permitted on the gym floor). A water bottle is recommended, though stations are available throughout the club. Pool users need swimwear and a swimming cap (mandatory for hygiene). You'll collect your access fob from reception on your first visit.

Club members receive 2 complimentary guest passes per month; Premium members receive 4. Passes provide full club access for the day. The hosting member must be present throughout. Essential members can bring guests at £20 per visit. All guests must sign a health declaration and be at least 16.

Yes — we support members across a wide range of recovery journeys. Our team includes sports rehabilitation and corrective exercise specialists. The hydrotherapy pool is frequently used as part of recovery protocols. We recommend bringing a GP or physio letter for your induction if you have a recent injury.

Payments

Monthly memberships are collected by Direct Debit. One-off payments (annual memberships, PT sessions, day passes, in-club purchases) accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or contactless. Payment details managed securely through the member app.

On the same calendar date each month as your original join date. If that falls on a weekend or bank holiday, it's collected on the next working day. A reminder email is sent three days before payment is due.

We notify you by email and retry after five working days. Access continues as normal during this period. If the second attempt fails, access is temporarily suspended until the balance is settled. Contact us as soon as possible if you anticipate a difficulty — we always aim to resolve these situations with a conversation, not a penalty.

Yes — 15% discount for full-time students (valid student ID or TOTUM card) and NHS employees (valid staff ID). Verified annually. Equivalent discounts for emergency services and armed forces personnel. Corporate rates available for 3+ employees — contact corporate@ironoak.co.uk.

Monthly payments are non-refundable once collected, as full access is provided throughout the billing period. Annual memberships are refundable within 14 days of joining if genuinely unsatisfied; a medical cancellation policy applies thereafter. Contact us immediately if you believe a payment was taken in error.
