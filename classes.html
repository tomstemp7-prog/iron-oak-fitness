What we offer

OUR CLASSES

Every class is coached, scalable, and welcoming — whether you're a beginner or a seasoned athlete.

HIIT

High-intensity interval training that burns fat, builds endurance, and keeps every session fresh. Expect circuits, partner work, and serious sweat.

45 min

Spin

Indoor cycling to the beat. Instructor-led rides with climbs, sprints, and intervals designed to push your cardiovascular limits.

50 min

Yoga Flow

A dynamic flow practice linking breath with movement. Builds flexibility, mobility, and mental clarity. All levels welcome.

60 min

Boxing Fit

Bag work, pad work, and conditioning drills. No fighting — just the ultimate full-body workout for all fitness levels.

45 min

Strength Foundations

Barbell-based strength work with a focus on technique. Squat, hinge, press, and pull — coached progressively for safe, consistent gains.

60 min

Pilates Core

Targeted core work and postural alignment. Improves stability, relieves back pain, and complements every other type of training.

45 min
